Essay support

Even the most talented and capable students can benefit from discussion and feedback in writing US college application essays. With so much riding on it, this unusual essay genre can feel daunting to write, especially for students coming from a different language and different school system. For many students, the personal, reflective essay US colleges ask for is something they have never been taught.

An original, clear essay does more than strengthen an application. It can prepare students to express themselves at a US university and help them feel more confident in the value they bring and what they will gain.

A note on generative AI: Given the challenges of writing these essays, some students (understandably) fall back on AI. Often this doesn't help them because even if the AI essay sounds fine, by nature, AI tools can only reproduce what has already been said. The world wants your unique voice and vision! My goal is to help students feel confident in sharing these.

Collaborative approach

I read each essay the way an admissions reader will, as someone meeting the student for the first time (and skimming hundreds of applications!). Then, as needed, I help the student to:

Clarify

Bring out what the student already knows about themselves and what matters to them.

Find and shape

Where needed, help the student discover their voice and their academic (and life) aspirations.

Add pop

Amplify what is strongest and most striking in what the student has written.

What I do

  • Help students brainstorm topics for the Common Application and college-specific essays.
  • Help students choose between topics, or combine them.
  • Consider the application package of essays as a whole and help identify overlaps or gaps.
  • Give feedback on drafts, with clear priorities.
  • Help students structure, cut to the word limit and polish.

What I don't do

  • Write students' essays. At all. (This is a discussion, feedback and light editing service, not ghost writing.)
  • Choose topics. The ideas are the student's.
  • Choose colleges or submit applications.
  • Guarantee admission. No one honestly can.
